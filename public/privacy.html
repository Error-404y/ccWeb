Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy describes how qzu. (Android package cc.qzu.app) handles information when you use the messaging app and its associated services.

1. Who operates qzu.

Developer and data controller: __QZU_OPERATOR__.

Privacy contact: __QZU_CONTACT__.

2. Information we process

  • Account and device information: a randomly assigned account identifier, device public key and associated identifier, authentication tokens or their hashes, hashed PINs, access status, and security settings. A phone number, email address and public username are not required to create an account.
  • Communications: messages, images, voice recordings, group membership, connection requests, contact preferences, blocks, message timestamps, delivery and read status, typing and presence information. We process this information to provide the features you use.
  • Support, abuse reports and feedback: messages you send to Support, submitted feedback, reset request references and relevant timestamps. Abuse reports include the reporting and reported account identifiers, chat and message references, selected reason, selected message text, explicitly submitted photos or voice messages, and optional evidence you explicitly submit; they are used for moderation. Feedback records do not include an account identifier, but your text may contain personal information.
  • Payment information: where the existing paid access feature is available, order references, plan, amount, payment status, submitted Litecoin, Bitcoin or Solana transaction IDs, redemption records and account or device associations. Public blockchain transactions are independently visible. Cryptocurrency checkout uses a public price feed and configured blockchain RPC providers or read-only public blockchain providers to verify payment transaction identifiers and amounts. We do not ask for wallet private keys. Payment tickets contain messages, timestamps and optional proof images you explicitly submit. They are accessible to authorized payment support staff and are not end-to-end encrypted. Never upload wallet recovery phrases, private keys or unrelated personal information.
  • Technical information: IP addresses and request metadata used to connect and limit abuse, security events and optional push registration tokens. App preferences and device credentials are also stored locally.

3. Permissions

Camera access is used to scan connection QR codes and, where supported, capture images you choose to send. Microphone access is used for voice messages. Images and audio you select or record are sent only as part of your chosen messaging action. Notification permission enables optional activity alerts. qzu. does not require access to your address book or precise location. You can revoke permissions in your device settings.

4. Why we process information

We use information to provide account access, deliver communications, maintain connections, protect accounts, prevent abuse, operate notifications, handle support, process access transactions and meet applicable legal obligations. Where applicable, processing is based on providing the requested service, legitimate security and operational interests, consent for optional features, or legal obligations. We do not use message content for advertising and do not sell personal data.

5. Encryption and security

Connections use HTTPS in production. Supported direct chats use an experimental device-key protocol with a manually compared safety code and encrypted message payloads. Group and Support chats use transport encryption; the server can access their content. The protocol has not been independently audited. Account PINs are stored as salted hashes and access is restricted by device authentication and account authorization. No security system can guarantee absolute protection.

6. Service providers and sharing

Oracle Cloud Infrastructure hosts the backend. Google Firebase Cloud Messaging processes device registration tokens and generic activity notifications when you enable Android notifications; these alerts do not include message content, sender or chat names. If configured, a push delivery provider may process equivalent notification information. Discord webhooks may receive explicitly submitted feedback, reset references and payment approval summaries for support operations. Recipients receive the content and metadata needed for your conversations. Authorized staff may access support records and operational data. Information may also be disclosed when legally required. Providers may process information outside your country; applicable transfer safeguards must be observed.

Provider information: Oracle, Firebase, Discord.

7. Retention and deletion

Ordinary chats are held temporarily in server memory, generally for 24 hours, subject to supported extensions or earlier deletion; temporary media follows the chat lifetime. Support conversations may remain in the server database until deleted or their selected timer expires. Payment ticket messages and proof are removed after seven days from closure, or after 90 days of inactivity for an open ticket. Rejected tickets are hidden from the staff dashboard after 12 hours; the underlying payment record is retained separately. Saved contacts are retained according to activity and contact settings. Signed public one-time encryption keys are kept for up to seven days to support offline chat initiation; private keys remain on registered devices. Account, payment and access records (including hashed recovery codes and administrator action logs), official posts and submitted feedback may remain until removed or no longer needed. Account security activity is limited to 50 events within 30 days. Notification tokens remain registered until removed or invalidated.

Removing a conversation removes its active service copy. Private backup tooling excludes support conversations, feedback and push tokens, but historical backups, recipient screenshots and external copies may remain. Account deletion removes the account row, credentials, device association, active chats, contacts, blocks, notification registrations, security activity and official read records. Limited transaction records are retained without account/device links to prevent reuse and support accounting obligations. Abuse reports and moderation records may retain voluntarily provided text and media evidence, moderator notes and enforcement history for up to 90 days; account links are removed on deletion. Device identifiers associated with safety suspensions may be retained for up to 90 days. We may retain records where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or security purposes and will explain applicable exceptions when handling your request.

8. Your choices and account deletion

You can disable optional notifications, change privacy settings, delete chats, block contacts and use Settings > Account > Delete account or the public account deletion page. You may generate an external deletion code and save it securely for later use. To request deletion of your account and associated personal data, or to request access, correction, restriction or other applicable privacy rights, contact the privacy contact above or use Settings > Help > Write Support. Include enough information to identify your account securely; never send your PIN, activation key or private device keys. We may need to verify ownership before acting. You may also contact your competent data protection authority.

9. Children

qzu. is intended for adults and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has supplied personal information, please contact us.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when our data practices change. The date at the top identifies the current version.
